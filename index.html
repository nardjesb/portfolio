Research project

Master's research — defended with distinction, Très Bien

Nano-therapy based on curcumin for the treatment of inflammatory bowel diseases

USTHB · March – June 2022 · Inter-laboratory collaboration between Cellular Pharmacology and Natural Gases laboratories

Development of a nanoparticulate system based on curcumin encapsulated in MgAl layered double hydroxide (MgAl-HDL-Cur) for the treatment of chronic inflammatory bowel diseases.

Work included

  • Synthesis by co-precipitation
  • Physicochemical characterization using XRD and FTIR
  • Biological validation using a murine model

Result

Significant reduction in oxidative stress and restoration of redox balance, with efficacy comparable to 5-FU.
