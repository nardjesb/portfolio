Research project

Master's research — Graduated with Très Bien

Nano-therapy based on curcumin for the treatment of inflammatory bowel diseases

USTHB · 2022 · Inter-laboratory research collaboration

Development and evaluation of a nanoparticulate system based on curcumin incorporated into MgAl layered double hydroxide (MgAl-HDL-Cur) for a potential therapeutic approach to chronic inflammatory bowel diseases.

Work included

  • Synthesis by co-precipitation
  • Physicochemical characterization using XRD and FTIR
  • Biological evaluation using a murine model
  • Assessment of oxidative stress and redox balance

Key finding

The experimental formulation was associated with a reduction in oxidative stress and restoration of redox balance, with an observed efficacy comparable to 5-FU in the experimental model.
